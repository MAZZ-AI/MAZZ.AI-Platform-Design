How it works

## Your menu becomes the script

No bots reading a generic script — MAZZ.AI is trained on your menu, hours, and delivery zones.

01 / ANSWER

### The call or chat comes in

MAZZ.AI answers in Arabic or English, on the first ring, every time — even during Friday night rush.

02 / HANDLE

### Orders, menu, delivery

It takes the order, answers menu and allergy questions, quotes delivery time, and confirms the total.

03 / HANDOFF

### A human steps in when needed

Complaints or anything outside its scope route straight to your staff, with full context. Custom orders stay with the AI.
