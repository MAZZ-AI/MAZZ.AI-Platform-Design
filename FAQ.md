## General

3

What does MAZZ.AI actually do?

MAZZ.AI answers your restaurant's phone calls and chats. It takes orders directly from your menu, answers questions about dishes, ingredients, and allergens, quotes delivery times and zones, and hands the conversation to your staff the moment it hits something it shouldn't handle alone.

Does it replace my staff?

No. MAZZ.AI is built to back up your team during the busiest hours, not replace them. Every pilot starts with 75% of calls going to a human, and even after the pilot, complaints and anything unusual still route to your staff. Custom and special-request orders are handled by the AI itself, not escalated.

What languages does it support?

Arabic and English, including customers who switch between the two mid-sentence — the way people actually talk in Cairo. The AI tracks language per sentence, not per call.

## Pilot & pricing

5

How does the free pilot work?

Every new restaurant gets a **3-month free pilot** at no cost. MAZZ.AI is deployed inside your real workflow — real calls, real orders — so you can measure its performance before paying anything.

What's the AI adoption roadmap during the pilot?

A gradual, human-backed ramp: **Month 1 is 25% AI / 75% human**, Month 2 is 50/50, Month 3 is 75% AI / 25% human, and after the pilot MAZZ.AI handles 100% of calls and chats — with handoff to a human always available.

What happens after the 3-month pilot?

You move to standard pricing: **6 EGP per minute**, billed monthly, with your first 1,000 minutes every month free for life. There's no contract during the pilot, and no obligation to continue.

How is the per-minute rate calculated?

You're billed for talk and chat time handled by MAZZ.AI, at 6 EGP per minute, after your first 1,000 free minutes each month. There are no setup fees, seat fees, or hidden charges.

Can I cancel during or after the pilot?

Yes. There's no contract during the 3-month pilot, and you can pause or cancel the service at any time afterward.

## Founding Partner Program

3

What is the Founding Partner Program?

The first **3 restaurants** to join MAZZ.AI become Founding Partners, not just clients. That means 12 months of maintenance and support, free development on features you request, and priority on everything we build next.

What does "free development" mean exactly?

We build the features Founding Partners request at no development cost. The only exception: if a requested feature increases the underlying per-minute cost to run, that specific increase is passed through — the development itself stays free.

How do I become a Founding Partner?

Apply for the pilot from the pricing page. Only 3 spots exist in total, so Founding Partner status is confirmed when you start your pilot, on a first-come basis.

## Technical & data

4

Can it integrate with my POS or delivery system?

Yes — orders taken by MAZZ.AI can be routed to your kitchen display, POS, or delivery workflow. During onboarding we map the integration to whatever system your restaurant already runs.

What happens when the AI can't handle a call?

It hands off to your staff immediately, with the full conversation and context attached, so your team never has to ask the customer to repeat themselves.

Is my customer data secure?

Call and chat data is used only to run and improve your restaurant's assistant. It isn't sold or shared with other businesses. Full detail is in our [Terms & Conditions](#).

How do I get started?

Apply for the free pilot from the pricing page. We'll set up your menu, hours, and delivery zones, then go live with the Month 1 ramp — 25% AI, 75% human, from day one.
