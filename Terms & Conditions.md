## 2The service

MAZZ.AI answers incoming phone calls and chat messages on your restaurant's behalf. The Service takes orders from the menu information you provide, answers questions about dishes, ingredients, and allergens, quotes delivery times and zones, and hands conversations to your staff when appropriate.

The Service is trained on the menu, hours, and policies you supply. You are responsible for keeping that information accurate and current; MAZZ.AI relies on it to respond to customers.

## 3The pilot program

New restaurants receive a **3-month free pilot** at no cost, during which the Service is deployed inside your real call and chat volume so you can measure its performance.

The pilot follows a fixed AI adoption roadmap, with a human available at every stage:

- **Month 1:** 25% of volume handled by AI, 75% by a human.
- **Month 2:** 50% AI, 50% human.
- **Month 3:** 75% AI, 25% human.
- **After the pilot:** 100% AI, with handoff to a human always available.

There is no contract and no fee during the 3-month pilot. You may stop using the Service at any point during this period without charge.

## 4Fees & billing
